Our Story

Sirohi Capital is a diversified group with businesses in Canada and India. We develop residential real estate in British Columbia, own and operate restaurant franchises for international brands in Canada, and hold a partner interest in Vardhman Electronics in India.

We have completed 15th Avenue, a development of 15 townhouses, and have developed several homes in the Mount Griffin subdivision in Vernon, BC. Next, we intend to enter upper-luxury hospitality by developing our own five-star hotel in Udaipur, Rajasthan, designed for destination weddings.

Hospitality · Udaipur, Rajasthan

A Five-Star Luxury Hotel in Udaipur

Sirohi Capital intends to enter the upper-luxury, five-star hospitality sector by developing and owning its own hotel. We plan to build the property in Udaipur, the City of Lakes, one of India's most celebrated places to marry.

The hotel will offer luxury stays of the highest standard, with a dedicated part of the property designed for destination weddings and the celebrations around them, from intimate ceremonies to multi-day festivities for families and their guests.
